About BiteGrow

Built by one person who does this for real clients — not an agency deck.

BiteGrow is run by Sanjay Meher, the same person you'll actually talk to on WhatsApp when you order a course.

I started as a video editor. Seven years in, I noticed the same pattern over and over with food businesses: the content was good, but nothing after it was built to catch the customer.

A great reel would get thousands of views and the business would still lose the order — because there was no fast way to actually buy, no one answering the DM in time, and no reason to show up again on Google search the next day. The bottleneck was never the content. It was everything downstream of it.

So I stopped treating video, ordering systems, automation, and local visibility as separate jobs for separate freelancers. BiteGrow is what happens when one person owns the whole chain — creative and tech — for a single kind of business: food.

It's not a rebrand of "I do everything." It's the opposite — a deliberately narrow focus on one industry, built deep enough that a restaurant, cloud kitchen, or D2C food brand can order exactly the piece they're missing, and know it was built by someone who understands the whole picture around it.
